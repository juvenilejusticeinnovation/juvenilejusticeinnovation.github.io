Our Mission

Research that connects law, policy, and practical reform.

Juvenile Justice Innovation Initiative LLC is a Michigan limited liability company established to develop and operate the National Juvenile Justice Legal Research & Resource Platform.

The Initiative produces independent legal scholarship, comparative research, model statutory language, and accessible policy resources for professionals, institutions, researchers, and communities working to improve outcomes for children and young people.
