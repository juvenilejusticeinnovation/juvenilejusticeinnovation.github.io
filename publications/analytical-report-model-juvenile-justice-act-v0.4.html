Analytical Report on the Model Juvenile Justice Act for States — Draft 4.0

Hanna Kotyk · Juvenile Justice Innovation Initiative LLC · August 2, 2026

Overview

This report analyzes the legal-policy architecture, theory of change, resource dependencies, implementation considerations, and evaluation design of Model Act Draft 4.0. Its primary audiences are scholars, legal professionals, universities, law clinics, researchers, educators, and organizations studying juvenile justice.

The report explains why Draft 4.0 expands the architecture to twelve linked modules, corrects technical ambiguities, adds safeguards that were absent or underdeveloped in v0.3, and replaces unsupported percentage projections with a baseline-driven measurement process.
