Codification & Juvenile Law

Incorporation as a Principle of Efficient Codification of Juvenile Laws

Hanna Kotyk · Original publication: November 19, 2021 · English-language research edition available

Overview

The effective codification of juvenile legislation requires the systematic organization and harmonization of existing laws and regulations. This study examines incorporation as both a method of legislative systematization and a practical principle for preparing a coherent Juvenile Code.

Incorporation preserves the substantive content of legal norms while arranging and updating the normative material in a structured collection. The article considers the relationship between incorporation and codification, the value of harmonizing fragmented legal provisions, and the role of careful preparatory systematization in producing legislation that is internally consistent, accessible, and capable of supporting children's rights.
