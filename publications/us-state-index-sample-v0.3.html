U.S. State Index — Sample (2 States) · v0.3

Hanna Kotyk · August 1, 2025

Overview

This historical sample covers New York and Texas and records the validation-stage structure used before the ten-state expansion. It preserves the development path of the U.S. State Juvenile Justice Statutory Index and should be read as a prior research milestone rather than the current edition.

The current Version 0.4 expands the work to ten states, replaces provisional descriptions with verified statutory citations and selected official institutional sources, and adds Model Act comparison notes and a verification protocol.
