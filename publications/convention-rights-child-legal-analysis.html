Convention on the Rights of the Child

Legal Composition and Algorithms of the Convention on the Rights of the Child: Legal-Technological Analysis

Hanna V. Kotyk · Juridical Scientific and Electronic Journal · 2021

Abstract

The article examines the legal composition of the Convention on the Rights of the Child through the methods of legal technology. It considers the Convention as an international agreement that translates protected interests into defined models of behavior, state obligations, safeguards, and implementation mechanisms.

The study analyzes the Convention's formal and substantive construction, the sequence and interaction of its legal algorithms, its drafting features, and its universal role in protecting children's rights and development. Particular attention is given to how the document's structure supports interpretation and practical implementation across different national legal systems.
