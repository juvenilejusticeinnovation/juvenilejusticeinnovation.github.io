Commentary to the Model Juvenile Justice Act for States — Draft 4.0

Hanna Kotyk · Juvenile Justice Innovation Initiative LLC · August 2, 2026

Overview

This Commentary explains the legal foundations, policy logic, drafting choices, and practical applications of Model Act Draft 4.0. It is intended primarily for scholars, legal professionals, universities, law clinics, researchers, educators, and organizations working in juvenile justice.

The Commentary accompanies the complete twelve-module framework and supports teaching, comparative legal analysis, practitioner education, and jurisdiction-specific application. It distinguishes binding constitutional or federal-law principles from proposed state-law protections and identifies points that require state adaptation.
