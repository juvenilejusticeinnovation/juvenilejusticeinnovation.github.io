Founder & Managing Director

Hanna Kotyk

Comparative legal researcher and policy author working at the intersection of juvenile justice, child protection, legislative systematization, and evidence-informed institutional reform.

Profile

Connecting comparative scholarship with practical legal-policy design.

Hanna Kotyk is the Founder and Managing Director of Juvenile Justice Innovation Initiative LLC, the Michigan-based legal research and publishing company developing the National Juvenile Justice Legal Research & Resource Platform.

Her research focuses on how legal systems organize, codify, and implement rules affecting children and young people. She examines juvenile justice and child-protection frameworks, international standards, legislative fragmentation, legal technique, and the design of practical tools that can support policy development and institutional decision-making.

Her current flagship project is the Model Juvenile Justice Act for States, a modular framework accompanied by detailed Commentary and an Analytical Report. The three-part suite connects model statutory language with interpretive guidance, implementation conditions, measurable indicators, and a pathway for state-level adaptation.

Research Focus

A coherent body of work, not isolated publications.

01

Juvenile justice reform

Research on age standards, diversion, restorative approaches, transfer to adult court, procedural safeguards, and measurable system performance.

02

Child protection and international standards

Analysis of children's rights, international legal standards, trafficking prevention, and national legal implementation.

03

Legislative systematization

Work on codification, consolidation, incorporation, legal composition, and the organization of fragmented normative material.

04

Practical policy tools

Development of model statutory language, explanatory commentary, implementation analysis, comparative references, and research resources.
